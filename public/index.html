BUILT FOR SIGNAL

Checks with restraint.

01
Outage or CAPTCHA?

Marked as an error or blocked state. It never becomes a content change or sends a change alert.

02
One page, many watchers?

Matching URLs due together share one browser fetch, then each monitor applies its own selectors and sensitivity.

03
Content that changes constantly?

Strip common volatile elements, normalize timestamps and tokens, then lower sensitivity or watch a smaller region.

04
How do checks stay polite?

Minimum 15-minute intervals, one queued browser job at a time, 5-second origin spacing, timeouts, and two retries.
